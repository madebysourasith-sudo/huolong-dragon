# Huolong — Ink and Colour

A fire dragon walks across a sheet of cold-press watercolour paper. Under its feet, a Chinese poem in black brush ink blooms into colour, and its breath burns real holes through the sheet.

**Live:** https://madebysourasith-sudo.github.io/huolong-dragon/

- 3D dragon modelled and rigged in Blender, running in three.js
- Paper, ink, watercolour bloom and burn are one WebGL shader
- One HTML file, no build step

Calligraphy font: [Ma Shan Zheng](https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Ma+Shan+Zheng) (SIL Open Font License), subset and embedded.

Made by Sourasith with Claude Code.
